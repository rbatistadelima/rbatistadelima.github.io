How to test

Lock-screen control: tap Start → lock the phone → press ⏮ (jump) and ⏭ (shoot) on the lock screen → unlock and check the counters. YouTube Music will stop when the demo starts.

Keep other audio: start YouTube Music playing → switch to this page → pick this mode → tap Start. YouTube Music should keep playing. The game is controlled only from the on-screen buttons above.

Hybrid (experimental): attempts to claim Now Playing and then immediately release it to ambient. May give a few seconds of lock-screen control while other audio keeps playing, or may fail entirely.
