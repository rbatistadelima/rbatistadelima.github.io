How It Works

  1. Upload a fully colored image (JPEG, PNG, or GIF).
  2. Convert to lineart to create a black-and-white outline version.
  3. Play the animation to watch the image being colored back in with diagonal brush strokes.
  4. Adjust settings like stroke speed and width to customize the animation.
  5. Export as GIF to save your timelapse animation.

The animation paints color by color (not region by region) using long diagonal brush strokes moving up-right (↗) across the canvas, simulating a human coloring a book with a marker.
